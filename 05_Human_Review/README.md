# Human Review

Human review strengthens this repository when the reviewer’s actual scope remains visible.

A transparent review record should state:

- reviewer name, when permission is given;
- relevant expertise or lived experience;
- conflicts, affiliations, or prior public positions;
- documents and pages actually examined;
- review lens and limits;
- findings, recommended actions, and unresolved questions.

Review does not imply endorsement of the book, repository, author, releasing authority, or any political conclusion. A narrow review should never be represented as a review of the entire corpus.

The most useful contribution is specific: identify the claim, open the source, show the page, explain the reasoning, and mark where the claim must stop.
